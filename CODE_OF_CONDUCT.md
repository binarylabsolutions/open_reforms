# Code of conduct

Open Reform is for people who want to improve how Pakistan works, whatever their politics.

## Expected

- Argue about ideas and evidence, not about people.
- Assume the other person also wants a better country.
- Welcome criticism of a proposal. Finding the flaw is a contribution.
- Admit it when a source proves you wrong.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, insults or harassment.
- Abuse aimed at any ethnic, religious, sectarian, regional or gender group.
- Party-political campaigning. Proposals are judged on evidence, not on who would get the credit.
- Invented sources or knowingly false numbers.
- Sharing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile.
