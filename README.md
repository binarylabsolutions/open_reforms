# Open Reform

Proven ideas from around the world, scored and proposed for Pakistan.

Open Reform is a YouTube channel and this public repository. For each reform we look at something that already works in another country, write a proposal for doing it in Pakistan, score it, and make a short video. Everything here is public, permanently.

Each reform is a starting point, not a finished answer. We are not political: we support no party and attack none, and anyone can implement what is here. Read the [Charter](CHARTER.md).

## See the reforms

Download or clone the repo and double-click **`index.html`**. No server, no install. It lists every reform, lets you filter by sector and by score, and opens each proposal when you click it.

## How a reform is scored

Every reform is rated **low / medium / high** on five metrics:

| Metric | Question | Good is |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | How much better does life get, and for how many people? | high |
| Cost | How much public money does it need? | low |
| Difficulty | How hard is it to build and run? | low |
| Resistance | How much political and vested-interest pushback? | low |
| Time | How long before citizens see results? | low |

These combine into a score out of 100. The best reforms are high impact, cheap, easy, unopposed and fast. Details: [.docs/metrics.md](.docs/metrics.md).

## No invented numbers

Every figure in a proposal carries a citation to a source that a human has opened and checked. A proposal cannot reach `proposal` status until an automatic check and a reviewer both pass it. A reform marked `research` is a draft and says so at the top.

## Contribute

One reform is one issue and one pull request. Suggest an idea, add a source, correct a number, or tell us why a reform would fail in Pakistan. You do not need to be a programmer.

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), and join the conversation on [Discord](https://discord.gg/wKVNcYcgq).

## Experts wanted

If you work in a field one of these reforms touches (transport, tax, energy, water, education, health, public administration), we want your view, including the reasons it would not work. We can set up a short call. See [Experts](CONTRIBUTING.md#experts).
